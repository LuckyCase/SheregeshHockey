About

A non-commercial project — we collect memories and photos

Sheregesh Hockey is a non-commercial project about the history of ice hockey in the village of Sheregesh. We collect memories, photos, and facts about teams, rinks, and tournaments from 1970–2000. If you have materials or stories — share them via «Share Your Story».

The Village

The mine, the people, and hockey's place in village life

Sheregesh is a village in Tashtagol district, Kemerovo region, built around a mine. Hockey on natural ice and on a boarded rink was a central part of local life for years and brought miners and families together. Both backyard hockey and the mine team «Gornyak» played here.

Teams

Gornyak and other teams

The main team was «Gornyak» of the Sheregesh mine; other teams took part in district and inter-village tournaments in different years. Lineups and photos will be added as materials are received.

Rinks & Venues

Outdoor rinks and the boarded rink

They played on natural ice by the mine, then on the boarded rink with lights. Schoolyards were used for backyard hockey. Specific places and descriptions will be added over time.
