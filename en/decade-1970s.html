1970s

The beginning of hockey in Sheregesh

First games on natural ice by the mine, construction of a rink with boards, creation of the «Gornyak» team, and the first inter-village tournament — key milestones of the decade.

  • 1971 — first games on natural ice by the mine
  • 1974 — construction of the first rink with boards
  • 1976 — creation of the mine team «Gornyak»
  • 1978 — first inter-village tournament
